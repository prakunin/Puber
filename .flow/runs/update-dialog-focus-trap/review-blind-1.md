### F1 [major] Starting a download sends focus to the content under the update dialog
- Where: app/src/main/java/com/kino/puber/ui/feature/main/component/MainScreenComponent.kt:108-110, together with app/src/main/java/com/kino/puber/core/ui/uikit/component/drawer/ModalNavigationDrawer.kt:281-282 and app/src/main/java/com/kino/puber/ui/feature/update/component/UpdatePromptOverlay.kt:98
- Problem: When the gate closes, the rail is put into `HandingOff` and stays there for as long as the dialog is up. The new comment says it is there to catch the focus bounce when the dialog is dismissed. But a bounce also happens without a dismiss. On `Available -> Downloading` the focused primary button leaves composition, and `DownloadingContent` has nothing focusable. Focus then falls to the rail (the comment itself describes this), and the rail is in `HandingOff`, so it calls `handoff.redirectFocusToContent()`. That is a plain `contentFocusRequester.requestFocus()`, and it ignores `LocalContentFocusActive`. The gate added to `ContentFocusHandoffEffect` does not cover this path. The new `onExit` trap in `TvDialogOverlay` does not help either, because focus is no longer inside the dialog.
- Failure scenario: The prompt opens over Main, the user presses "Update", and the state becomes `Downloading`. Focus lands on a card behind the scrim. While the download runs, D-pad moves through the hidden rows, and Enter opens a screen under the modal. The same happens on any later state change that removes the focused button. Base had the same gap (the rail opened instead), but this diff is meant to close it and makes it reach the content.
- Fix: Do not redirect while the gate is closed. Either have `redirectFocusToContent` check the gate (pass `outerActive` into `ContentFocusHandoff`, or skip the redirect when it is false), or give the dialog a focus target that never leaves composition, such as a focusable card or a focus target in `DownloadingContent` and `CheckingContent`, so focus never drops out of the overlay.

### F2 [minor] While the check is running, focus stays on the screen behind the dialog and the rail sits in `HandingOff`

- Problem: `Checking` counts as open, so the screens are gated and Main starts a handoff. But the overlay never takes focus in `Checking`: there is nothing focusable, and the request is skipped on purpose. Focus stays on the item under the scrim, and the drawer is held in `HandingOff` with no one to settle it until the gate opens.
- Failure scenario: A manual "check for updates" from Settings (VM line 132) shows `Checking`. During the check, D-pad and Enter still act on the Settings item under the scrim, so a second Enter starts another check. D-pad Left into the rail is bounced straight back by the `HandingOff` redirect, and the rail does not open.
- Fix: Either do not close the gate in `Checking`, or give the Checking content a focus target that it takes.
