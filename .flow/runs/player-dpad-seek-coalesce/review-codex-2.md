The flushed seek therefore opens no new 250 ms window. Scenario: the leading seek is at 0 ms, the ended callback commits at 100 ms, and a held key presses again at 150 ms. That press becomes pending, and the old window then fires a trailing seek at 250 ms. That makes three exact seeks in 250 ms, with the last two 150 ms apart. This is a spacing overshoot of one extra seek, on a rare path: the held key has to reach the end of the media. It does not break correctness, so it is minor. A fix would be for the flush to restart the window, or to reset `seekThrottleJob` when it commits.
